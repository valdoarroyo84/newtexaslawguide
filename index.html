Education

Public school funding and education

House Bill 2 · Different sections have different effective dates

In plain English: HB 2 makes broad changes to public education and school finance. Some provisions took effect in 2025; certain provisions are scheduled to take effect on September 1, 2026, with others later.

  • Families and school staff may want to check which provisions affect their school or program.
  • The law has multiple effective dates, so “HB 2 is effective” does not mean every section started at once.

Education

Special education funding

Senate Bill 568 · Additional sections effective September 1, 2026

In plain English: SB 568 changes parts of how special education is funded in Texas public schools. Some sections took effect earlier, while specified sections are scheduled for September 1, 2026.

  • The law concerns public-school special education funding.
  • It does not mean every student’s services or eligibility automatically change in the same way.

Consumer & business

Residential solar sales rules

Senate Bill 1036 · Certain requirements effective September 1, 2026

In plain English: SB 1036 creates a state regulatory framework for residential solar transactions, including registration requirements for certain people or businesses selling solar products and services.

  • Homeowners considering solar should verify a seller’s credentials and read the contract carefully.
  • Requirements may depend on the role of the seller and the transaction.
